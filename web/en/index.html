FAQ

Which browser works best?

Any modern browser on phone or computer — Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox. Reels build faster on a computer.

Do I need to install anything?

No. VideoRils runs right on the page and builds the reel on your device — nothing is uploaded to a server.

How much does it cost?

Your first reel is free. After that — a subscription for 7, 30 or 365 days in the “License” window, priced in US dollars and paid in USDT. The key is inserted automatically and works in this browser.

Where is the finished reel saved?

Tap “Download reel” — the file goes to Downloads. Your browser remembers keys and settings.

Do I need to download anything heavy?

No. Only some features download a model once — the Piper voice (~63 MB) or speech recognition for “From video”. After that they load from the browser’s memory.
