questions

Quick answers

I can’t edit video — can I do it?+

Yes, no editing at all. You type a topic — VideoRils writes the script, voices it, picks the footage and adds subtitles. You get a finished vertical mp4 and never open a timeline.

How do I try it for free?+

Tap “Start creating” — VideoRils opens right in your browser, on phone or computer. Your first reel is free: our keys are already connected, nothing to sign up for and no card needed. Like it — get a subscription.

Do I have to pay for AI?+

No. Our free AI (Groq and Gemini), free voices and stock footage are already connected — nothing to set up. Premium is optional, with your own keys: lifelike ElevenLabs voices and Claude scripts. The VideoRils subscription pays for VideoRils itself, not for AI.

Where does the footage come from?+

VideoRils picks clips and photos for every line from the free libraries Pexels and Pixabay — nothing to connect, it already works. Footage authors are credited under the scenes and under the finished reel. You can replace any scene with your own photo or video from the gallery.

How do I pay and get a key?+

Pay by card right on the site. Prices are shown in US dollars at our exchange rate. After payment the key arrives and is inserted automatically — nothing to copy by hand.

How does billing work?+

A subscription for a period — 7 days, 30 days or a year. No auto-renewal: pay for the period, use it, renew yourself if you want. The key arrives and is inserted automatically, and a couple of days before the end VideoRils reminds you to renew.

What languages does it support?+

Reels in English or Russian: tick “Reel in English” and the script, voice and subtitles are in English. The interface switches between EN and RU.

Who is responsible for uploaded content?+

VideoRils is a processing tool. Users upload and publish videos and texts and are responsible for that content. Only upload what you have the rights to. VideoRils does not download videos from platforms — it only works with a file you choose yourself.
