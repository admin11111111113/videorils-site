How to make faceless YouTube Shorts without filming

Faceless channels grow on ideas, not on faces. If you can come up with topics, VideoRils turns each of them into a finished Short — script, footage, voice-over and captions — without a camera.

Why faceless Shorts work

Viewers on Shorts decide in the first second. A strong hook, clear visuals and readable captions matter more than who is talking. Facts, top-5 lists, history, money tips, “did you know” and motivation are classic faceless formats that keep working.

Make one in a minute

  1. Type a topic — one line is enough: “3 mistakes first-time home buyers make” or “why cats love boxes”.
  2. Tap “Get result”. The AI writes a short script with a hook and splits it into scenes, then picks a matching vertical clip for every line.
  3. Check the scenes. Swap any clip, upload your own photo or video, trim, or regenerate a line.
  4. Pick a voice and subtitles — free natural voices, your own recording, or ElevenLabs with your key.
  5. Tap “Voice & build reel” and download a ready 9:16 MP4 for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

Post every day without burning out

The hard part of a faceless channel is consistency. In Lite you can enter up to five topics and get five reels in one go, preview every script and clip, then build them all. “💡 Ideas of the day” gives you fresh topics when you run dry.

Make it yours

  • use “My voice” to record your own narration with a teleprompter;
  • add your channel logo (Lite);
  • pick a caption style that becomes your signature;
  • end every Short with a call to action and a QR code.

Frequently asked questions

Can a faceless Shorts channel be monetized?
Yes, YouTube monetizes faceless channels the same way as others, as long as the content is original and follows YouTube policies. Do not re-upload other people’s videos.
Is the stock footage safe to use?
The clips come from Pexels, Pixabay and Wikimedia Commons with their licenses; the app credits the authors. Check each license if you need specific rights.
How long should a Short be?
Most strong Shorts are 20–40 seconds. Mini makes up to 40 seconds, Lite up to 120.
Do I need my own voice?
No. Free natural voices are built in. You can also record yourself or use ElevenLabs with your key.
Can I make several Shorts at once?
Yes, in Lite: up to five topics in one batch.
