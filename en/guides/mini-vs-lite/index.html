Mini vs Lite: which VideoRils is right for you

Both make reels from text in your browser. The difference is how far you want to go.

Mini

  • reels up to 40 seconds;
  • AI script, clips, voice-over, captions;
  • final frame with QR;
  • “From a video” with stock footage;
  • “My voice”;
  • one own key per service.

Lite

  • everything in Mini;
  • reels up to 120 seconds;
  • Batch — up to 5 reels at once, and series;
  • clip-mix cut to music;
  • “From a video” on the original footage;
  • hook cover, animated captions, logo;
  • Auto-editing of your own video;
  • up to 5 own keys per service.
